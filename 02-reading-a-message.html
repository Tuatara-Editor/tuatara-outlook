Reading a message

The message, its attachments, and how to see it as a mail program shows it.

2.2 Attachments

RET on an attachment opens it in Tuatara from a copy only you can read. Nothing in it is run.

O opens it in the desktop's own program for its kind, after asking. The copy is private to you, and only a kind that may be opened outside Tuatara is opened: a picture, a PDF, a sound, a video or an Office document. That program is not shut in the sandbox.

A part that cannot be read (an attachment, a value) is said so in the message, in lines that Reading note shows when the message is opened. An attachment whose contents are missing is a short text of that name saying so.

A message attached to a message is shown as a message. How many levels deep this goes is set by *outlook-nesting-limit*; deeper ones are listed as not read. A message and the messages attached to it and to those are limited to 256, so that a small file cannot make the reader take memory or time without end.

2.3 Seeing a message as it looks

P in a message draws it as a mail program shows it. Its HTML is laid out as on a screen, with its colours, tables and fonts and the pictures it carries in their places. Above it are its headers (from, to, subject, dates, attachments) as a mail program prints them, and its internet headers, whole as they came, are on a page of their own after it. The pages are shown in Tuatara's PDF view, with its keys (zoom, search, the text).

The HTML comes from the message itself: the HTML part of a saved mail, an Outlook item's HTML, or the HTML its rich text keeps. A message of plain text alone looks as it is shown already.

It is drawn by WeasyPrint, shut in Tuatara's sandbox with no network, so nothing is fetched from the internet (a picture, a style, a tracking pixel). The line over the pages says how many such things the message holds. WeasyPrint runs no scripts at all. It sees only the message's HTML and the pictures it shows, written into a directory of your cache only you can read, and your fonts, read only.

The program is named by *outlook-page-program*. The pages need poppler's pdftoppm too, as any PDF does. The packages that provide WeasyPrint are: weasyprint on Fedora, Debian and Ubuntu, python-weasyprint on Arch and python3-weasyprint on openSUSE.

2.4 Attachments earlier in a conversation

A reply or a forward quotes the earlier mails of its conversation but seldom carries their attachments. When the mailbox holds those earlier mails, a message opened from its list ends with "Attachments earlier in this conversation". Each attachment is shown under the subject, sender and date of the message it came with, and how it was found (by In-Reply-To, by References, or by the conversation index of the header or of the Outlook item). It says too when one Message-ID is given to several messages. RET on one opens it as the message's own attachments open, from a copy only you can read, shut in the sandbox, nothing in it run.

The earlier mails are found by the Message-IDs the message's In-Reply-To and References headers name, and theirs in turn. When those find none, they are found by Outlook's conversation index (the Thread-Index header, or the PR_CONVERSATION_INDEX of the Outlook item). Its first 22 octets name the conversation and each reply adds 5, so a message whose index is the start of this one's is earlier in the same conversation. The links are read as the mailbox is listed and looked up in tables made once. Reading the earlier mails is part of reading the message, in the background.

In the Enron sample mailbox (71 messages) every message has a conversation index, none has In-Reply-To or References, and two find an earlier message.
