#+title: Settings and limits
#+language: en

* Settings and limits

How much of a file outlook reads, and what it leaves unread.

outlook reads files from anywhere, some made to do harm. Everything it reads is
held to a size or a time, so that no file can make it run without end or fill
the memory. What it leaves unread, it says in the list or the message.

** The settings

Set them in the package's =:config= in your init file:

#+begin_src lisp
(load-package "outlook"
              :source "https://github.com/Tuatara-Editor/tuatara-outlook"
              :config
              (setf *outlook-include-deleted* nil))
#+end_src

~C-h v~ on a setting shows its help once the package is loaded. A size is a
whole number of octets, or words such as =512M= or =64G=, in units of 1024. A
time is a number of seconds, or words such as =90m= or =2h=.

#+table: settings *outlook-page-program* *outlook-include-deleted* *outlook-largest-file* *outlook-nesting-limit* *outlook-mailbox-seconds* *outlook-mailbox-most-files* *outlook-search-text-limit* *outlook-mailbox-space* *outlook-preview*

An item or saved mail larger than =*outlook-largest-file*= opens as any file. A
message of a mailbox reads no more of the mailbox than this. A mailbox is
stopped after =*outlook-mailbox-seconds*=, or when it has written
=*outlook-mailbox-space*=, or when the drive has less than 1 GB free, and what
was read by then is listed. Past =*outlook-mailbox-most-files*= the rest are
left out, and the list says so.

** Fixed limits

These are not settings:

- a message is read with no more than an eighth of the reader's memory;
- at most 256 messages attached to a message and to those;
- a message of parts within parts is read as deep as twice
  =*outlook-nesting-limit*=;
- folders more than 64 deep are listed under =Orphans=;
- a folder whose path would pass 1,024 octets has its name cut.

** What outlook does not do

- It does not read the offline mailboxes of Outlook 2013 and later (=.ost= of
  pages of 4 KB, blocks compressed). It refuses them and says so.
- It does not read a mailbox without the sandbox (=bwrap=).
- It never changes a mailbox, an item or a saved mail, or its access time.
- It does not fetch anything a message points to, and runs nothing in it.
